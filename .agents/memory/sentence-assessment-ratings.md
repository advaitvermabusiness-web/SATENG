---
name: Sentence assessment ratings
description: Product rule for evaluating learner-authored vocabulary sentences.
---

Sentence assessment has two independent dimensions: grammar and complexity. Grammar covers sentence structure, agreement, tense, word forms, articles, and clarity; complexity covers the sophistication and structural variety of the sentence.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants grammar feedback without changing or conflating the existing complexity assessment.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing complexity score, labels, and feedback contract. Add grammar as a separate 1–5 rating with its own label and feedback in every sentence-check response and display it alongside—not instead of—the complexity result.
