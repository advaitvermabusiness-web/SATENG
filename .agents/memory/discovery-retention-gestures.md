---
name: Discovery retention gestures
description: Meaning and persistence rules for the Discovery swipe meter.
---

Discovery uses one Tinder-style retention meter rather than three visible rating buttons: left means the learner needs help, right means they understand the gist but want more information, and up means they know the word completely.

**Why:** The directions express different support needs, not merely generic confidence levels. A fully known word should not keep returning in the Discovery feed.

**How to apply:** Left schedules a quick review, right schedules a next-day review, and up records full confidence and retires the word from future Discovery batches. Preserve keyboard access for all three directions.

Keep this gesture model limited to Discovery. Learn and Quick Mode use explicit rating buttons rather than horizontal swipe actions.
