---
name: Audience mode boundary
description: Product distinction between ongoing vocabulary learners and SAT-only learners.
---

Everyday vocabulary is the default path for learners building a lasting habit; SAT Mode is the focused path for SAT test takers. Onboarding should ask this intent directly, and each mode should keep its copy, word sources, practice, saved words, and progress aligned with that choice.

**Why:** Mixing the two audiences makes the app’s purpose unclear and weakens the reason each group has to return.

**How to apply:** Keep long-term learners in useful everyday vocabulary by default. Use SAT Mode for exam-focused words and study language, and make switching explicit rather than silently mixing the paths.
