---
name: Learning mode boundary
description: Product distinction between the guided Learn lesson and Quick Mode.
---

Learn is the guided lesson: reveal the word, connect context and word relationships, use the toolkit, complete active recall, then rate confidence. Quick Mode is for fast browse-and-review.

**Why:** Without a deliberate lesson checkpoint, both surfaces become the same word card with different navigation.

**How to apply:** Add depth, sequencing, or practice to Learn; keep Quick concise and optimized for rapid review. Do not reintroduce horizontal rating swipes to either mode.

Learn’s practice checkpoint includes an AI sentence assessor that reports both effective word usage and a 1–5 sentence-complexity rating with feedback.
