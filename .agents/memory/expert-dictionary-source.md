name: Expert dictionary source
description: Product boundary and reference-source decision for the advanced vocabulary track.
---

Keep Expert Mode distinct from both Everyday vocabulary and SAT preparation. Use Merriam-Webster as the external dictionary reference for Expert words.

**Why:** The product is aimed primarily at American learners, and the user selected Merriam-Webster for that audience.

**How to apply:** New Expert vocabulary and related reference links should use American English conventions and point to Merriam-Webster without copying proprietary definitions.
